Chronoface

An analog clock screensaver and live wallpaper for macOS.

The installer picks the right variant for your system automatically:

  • macOS 14 Sonoma and later: Chronoface.app in Applications plus a screensaver extension.
  • macOS 13 Ventura: the legacy .saver in /Library/Screen Savers/.

After installation, open System Settings → Screen Saver and pick Chronoface in the Other section.

To customize themes, colors and a custom background on macOS 14+, launch the Chronoface app from Spotlight.
